Study RPG v1.3.3 Dataset Source Note
=====================================

MCQ and written questions in this build are imported from the user-uploaded file:
study_rpg_dataset.zip

Import rules used:
- MCQ and written questions are kept as separate datasets.
- Questions are mapped by class, group, subject, chapter, and difficulty.
- Same-subject duplicate questions are removed.
- Same-subject cross-difficulty duplicates are removed.
- Easy, medium, and hard buckets are preserved where present and balanced where the uploaded dataset was missing a difficulty bucket.
- Mission GK questions use a reserved dataset subset that is not inserted into subject MCQ bank to avoid repeated app questions.
- No blank source-only question bank is used in this version.
